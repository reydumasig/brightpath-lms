P1

Offboarding Checklist · 25 min · 1 page max

Scenario. You support a disability services provider with about 175 employees. The company uses Google Workspace for email, files, and calendar, plus several third-party apps (for example HR, scheduling, training, CRM, and AI tools). An employee was terminated today at 2 PM.

Task. Write a one-page offboarding checklist for Google Workspace and connected third-party apps. Write it so that a non-technical office manager could follow it without calling you.

Your checklist should be clear about the order of steps, what to do with the person's data, and what to record as evidence that each step was done. Use placeholders for company-specific details you cannot know.
